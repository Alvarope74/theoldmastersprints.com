Édouard Manet: biography, paintings and prints

París, 1832 – París, 1883

French painter who brought modern life into high art and paved the way for Impressionism. An admirer of Velázquez and Goya, he caused scandals with Luncheon on the Grass and Olympia, although he never exhibited with the Impressionists.
