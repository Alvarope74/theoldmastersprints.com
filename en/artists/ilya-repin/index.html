Ilya Repin: biography, paintings and prints

Chuguyiv (Ucrania), 1844 – Kuokkala (hoy Repino), 1930

Ilya Repin, the leading Russian Realist painter of the 19th century and a member of the Peredvizhniki or “Wanderers”. He painted historical scenes and the life of the Russian people, such as Barge Haulers on the Volga.
