Famous paintings at the Art Institute of Chicago: 2 masterpieces to see

The Art Institute of Chicago was founded in 1879 and has occupied its Michigan Avenue building since 1893. It is one of the great museums of the United States, with an outstanding Impressionist collection and American classics.

Must-see works

American Gothic

Grant Wood · 1930

A stern farmer holding a pitchfork and a younger woman stand in front of a white wooden house with a Gothic Revival window.

A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte

Georges Seurat · 1884–1886

Parisians of different classes spend a Sunday on the island of La Grande Jatte in the Seine: strollers, a woman with a monkey on a leash, a girl in white, boatmen and rowers.
