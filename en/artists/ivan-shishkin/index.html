Ivan Shishkin: biography, paintings and prints

Yelábuga (Rusia), 1832 – San Petersburgo, 1898

Russian painter and printmaker, the great landscapist of his country’s forests. The son of a grain merchant from Yelabuga, he studied at the Moscow School of Painting, Sculpture and Architecture and at the Imperial Academy of Arts in St Petersburg, where he won the large gold medal in 1860; he then trained in Munich, Zurich and Düsseldorf. He was one of the founding members of the Peredvizhniki (the Itinerants) and a member of the Society of Russian Etchers. He died suddenly at his easel.
