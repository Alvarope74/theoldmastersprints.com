About us

TheOldMastersPrints is a small shop that reproduces some of the world’s best-loved paintings, from the Prado, the Louvre, the Rijksmuseum and other great museums, so you can hang them at home.

Every work is in the public domain and is printed from a high-resolution image of the original. Alongside each one we publish a page explaining what it shows, where it hangs and its key facts, so you know exactly what you are hanging.

How it works

  • Prints are made to order in the Czech Republic and reach the whole EU in 3 to 8 days, with free shipping and no customs.
  • Canvases are stretched on pine frames and made to order in the USA, with free shipping to most of the EU and VAT included.
  • Sizes follow the proportions of the original: if the paper differs, the painting is centred with a white border instead of being cropped.

Sales take place on Etsy. See new works on Pinterest.
