Albrecht Dürer: biography, paintings and prints

Núremberg, 1471 – Núremberg, 1528

Albrecht Dürer, the German painter, printmaker and theorist who became a leading figure of the Northern Renaissance. The son of a Nuremberg goldsmith, he was apprenticed in 1486 to Michael Wolgemut and travelled to Italy, where he came to know the work of Giovanni Bellini. His woodcuts made him famous across Europe while still in his twenties, and from 1512 he worked for Emperor Maximilian I. He brought classical motifs and the nude into Northern art and wrote treatises on perspective and human proportion.
