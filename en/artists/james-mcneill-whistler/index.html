James McNeill Whistler: biography, paintings and prints

Lowell (Massachusetts), 1834 – Londres, 1903

American painter and printmaker who spent almost all his life in Europe. He lived part of his childhood in Saint Petersburg, was dismissed from the West Point military academy and trained in Paris before settling in London in 1859. He championed art for art’s sake and titled his paintings like pieces of music: arrangements, symphonies and nocturnes. In 1878 he sued the critic John Ruskin for libel; he won, but was awarded only a farthing in damages, and the case left him bankrupt.
