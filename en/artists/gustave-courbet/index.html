Gustave Courbet: biography, paintings and prints

Ornans, 1819 – La Tour-de-Peilz (Suiza), 1877

French painter and leader of Realism, who wanted to paint only what could be seen. He took part in the Paris Commune of 1871 and died in exile in Switzerland.
