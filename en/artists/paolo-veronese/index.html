Paolo Veronese: biography, paintings and prints

Verona, 1528 – Venecia, 1588

Venetian Renaissance painter known for his great banquets and religious scenes full of colour, architecture and figures. He was summoned before the Inquisition in 1573 over the profane details of a Last Supper.
