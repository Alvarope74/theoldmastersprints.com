Famous paintings at the Musée d'Orsay: 7 masterpieces to see

The Musée d'Orsay occupies the former Gare d'Orsay railway station, built for the 1900 World's Fair, and opened as a museum in 1986. It covers art from 1848 to 1914, which makes it home to the world's largest collection of Impressionist painting.

Must-see works

Luncheon on the Grass

Édouard Manet · 1863

Two fashionably dressed men talk in a wood next to a naked woman who looks out at the viewer; in the background another woman bathes in a stream.

Bal du moulin de la Galette

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1876

A Sunday afternoon at the Moulin de la Galette, an open-air dance garden in Montmartre.

Olympia

Édouard Manet · 1863

A naked woman reclines on a bed and looks straight at the viewer without modesty.

A Burial at Ornans

Gustave Courbet · 1849–1850

A funeral in Ornans, Courbet’s home town in the Franche-Comté.

The Birth of Venus

William-Adolphe Bouguereau · 1879

Venus, naked, rises on a shell drawn by dolphins, arms raised to gather her hair.

Whistler's Mother

James McNeill Whistler · 1871

Anna McNeill Whistler, the painter’s mother, sits in profile on a chair in a long black dress and a white lace cap, her hands folded in her lap over a handkerchief.

The Painter's Studio

Gustave Courbet · 1854–1855

Courbet painted himself at the centre of his Paris studio, seated at an easel, painting a landscape of his native Franche-Comté.
