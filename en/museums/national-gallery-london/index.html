Famous paintings at the National Gallery: 2 masterpieces to see

The National Gallery in London was founded in 1824 and has stood on Trafalgar Square since 1838. It holds European painting from the 13th to the 20th century, and entry to the permanent collection is free.

Must-see works

Arnolfini Portrait

Jan van Eyck · 1434

Giovanni di Nicolao Arnolfini, an Italian merchant living in Bruges, and his wife stand in a room.

The Ambassadors

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1533

Two French diplomats in London, Jean de Dinteville and Georges de Selve, stand life-size beside shelves full of scientific instruments, a globe, books, a lute with a broken string and a Lutheran hymnbook.
