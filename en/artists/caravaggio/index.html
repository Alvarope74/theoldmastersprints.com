Caravaggio: biography, paintings and prints

Milán, 1571 – Porto Ercole, 1610

Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio, the Italian painter who revolutionised painting in the late 16th and early 17th centuries. He trained in Milan under Simone Peterzano and settled in Rome around 1592, where his large religious canvases, painted from life in strong chiaroscuro, made his name. His life was violent: in 1606 he killed Ranuccio Tomassoni in a brawl and fled to Naples, Malta and Sicily. He died in 1610, aged 38, on his way back to Rome. His influence, known as Caravaggism, spread across Europe, from Rubens to Velázquez and Rembrandt.
