Francisco Goya: biography, paintings and prints

Fuendetodos, 1746 – Burdeos, 1828

Francisco de Goya, painter to the Spanish court who portrayed the royal family and, after the war against Napoleon and an illness that left him deaf, painted some of the harshest images in art, such as The Disasters of War and the Black Paintings. He died in exile in Bordeaux.
