Famous paintings at the Museo del Prado: 5 masterpieces to see

The Prado opened in Madrid in 1819 with the Spanish royal collections. It is the place to see Velázquez, Goya and Hieronymus Bosch, and one of the finest collections of European painting in the world.

Must-see works

Las Meninas

Diego Velázquez · 1656

The Infanta Margarita, daughter of Philip IV, stands in the centre surrounded by her ladies-in-waiting (meninas) María Agustina Sarmiento and Isabel de Velasco, the dwarfs Mari Bárbola and Nicolasito Pertusato and a mastiff.

The Garden of Earthly Delights

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1490–1510

It is a triptych.

The Third of May 1808

Francisco Goya · 1814

It recreates the execution of Madrid civilians by Napoleon’s troops in the early hours of 3 May 1808, after the uprising of the previous day.

The Surrender of Breda

Diego Velázquez · 1634–1635

On 5 June 1625, after a long siege, the Dutch governor Justin of Nassau hands the keys of Breda to General Ambrogio Spinola, commander of the Spanish army.

Saturn Devouring His Son

Francisco Goya · c. 1819–1823

The Titan Saturn, Cronus in Greek mythology, devours one of his children so that none will overthrow him, as had been prophesied.
