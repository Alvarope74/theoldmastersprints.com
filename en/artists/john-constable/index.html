John Constable: biography, paintings and prints

East Bergholt (Suffolk), 1776 – Londres, 1837

English landscape painter and one of the great figures of Romanticism. The son of a wealthy corn merchant who owned Flatford and Dedham mills, he persuaded his father in 1799 to let him study at the Royal Academy Schools. He painted above all Dedham Vale on the River Stour, the countryside of his childhood now known as “Constable Country”. He was never financially successful and sold more in France than in England; in France he inspired the Barbizon school. He was not elected to the Royal Academy until the age of 52.
