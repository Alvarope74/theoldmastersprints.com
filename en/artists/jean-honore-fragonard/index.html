Jean-Honoré Fragonard: biography, paintings and prints

Grasse, 1732 – París, 1806

French painter and one of the great masters of the Rococo. A pupil of François Boucher, he won the Prix de Rome in 1752 and spent several years in Italy. He painted gallant scenes, fantasy portraits and landscapes with quick, luminous brushwork. After the Revolution his style fell out of fashion and he died almost forgotten.
