Sandro Botticelli: biography, paintings and prints

Florencia, c. 1445 – Florencia, 1510

Alessandro di Mariano Filipepi, known as Botticelli, a painter of the Florentine Renaissance. The nickname, "little barrel", came from his elder brother Giovanni. He probably trained with Filippo Lippi and by 1470 had his own workshop in Florence. He worked for the Medici and their circle, and in 1481–1482 he was one of the painters summoned to Rome to decorate the walls of the Sistine Chapel. His great mythologies, such as Primavera and The Birth of Venus, are among the most famous images of the Renaissance. In his later years he painted mostly religious subjects, at a time marked by the preaching of Savonarola. He died almost forgotten and was rediscovered in the 19th century, above all by the Pre-Raphaelites.
