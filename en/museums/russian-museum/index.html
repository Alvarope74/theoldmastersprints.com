Famous paintings at the Russian Museum: 3 masterpieces to see

The State Russian Museum in Saint Petersburg opened in 1898 in the Mikhailovsky Palace and was the first state museum of Russian art. It holds major 19th-century canvases by painters such as Repin and Aivazovsky.

Must-see works

Reply of the Zaporozhian Cossacks

Ilya Repin · 1880–1891

A crowd of Zaporozhian Cossacks gathers round a table where a scribe is writing their answer to the Ottoman sultan Mehmed IV, who had demanded their submission.

The Ninth Wave

Ivan Aivazovsky · 1850

Dawn after a night of storm.

Barge Haulers on the Volga

Ilya Repin · 1870–1873

Eleven men, harnessed in leather straps, drag a barge along the sandy bank of the Volga under a blazing sun.
