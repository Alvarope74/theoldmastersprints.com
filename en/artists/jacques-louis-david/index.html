Jacques-Louis David: biography, paintings and prints

París, 1748 – Bruselas, 1825

The leading painter of French Neoclassicism. He moved from subjects of Antiquity to the Revolution, which he actively supported, and then became Napoleon’s official painter. He died in exile in Brussels after the Restoration.
