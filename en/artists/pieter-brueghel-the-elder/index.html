Pieter Brueghel the Elder: biography, paintings and prints

h. 1525-1530 – Bruselas, 1569

Pieter Bruegel the Elder, a Flemish painter famous for his landscapes and scenes of peasant life, full of detail and humour. He founded a dynasty of painters with his sons Pieter the Younger and Jan.
