Edgar Degas: biography, paintings and prints

París, 1834 – París, 1917

French painter, sculptor and printmaker, one of the founders of Impressionism, although he rejected the label and preferred to call himself a realist. The son of a banker, he trained at the École des Beaux-Arts and by copying the old masters in the Louvre and in Italy. He did not paint outdoors, preferring cafés, theatres, the racecourse and above all the ballet, which appears in more than half of his works. He was a master of pastel and, as his eyesight began to fail, he modelled figures in wax.
