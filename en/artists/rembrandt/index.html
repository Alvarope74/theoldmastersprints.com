Rembrandt: biography, paintings and prints

Leiden, 1606 – Ámsterdam, 1669

Rembrandt van Rijn, the great painter of the Dutch Golden Age. He made his name in Amsterdam as a portraitist and painter of biblical stories, with dramatic chiaroscuro and an exceptional grasp of psychology. His self-portraits, nearly eighty of them, span his whole life.
