Gifts for art lovers: ideas by budget and by person

A reproduction of a famous painting is a personal, lasting gift, especially if the work connects with the person receiving it: their profession, their city or their story as a couple.

By budget

BudgetOption
Under €25A4 or A3 print of almost any work
€25-50A2 or A1 print for a main wall
€50-120Ready-to-hang canvas, no frame needed

Prices include VAT and shipping to the EU is free.

By person

  • Doctors and medical students: The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp.
  • Philosophy or history teachers: The Death of Socrates.
  • Couples and anniversaries: The Kiss or The Jewish Bride.
  • Weddings: Arnolfini Portrait.
  • Lovers of the Prado and Madrid: Las Meninas.
  • Van Gogh fans: The Starry Night.
  • The person who has everything: The Ambassadors and its hidden skull, which makes for a good story.
