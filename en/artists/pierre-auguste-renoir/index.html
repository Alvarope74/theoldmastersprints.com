Pierre-Auguste Renoir: biography, paintings and prints

Limoges, 1841 – Cagnes-sur-Mer, 1919

One of the founders of Impressionism. He began as a porcelain painter and became famous for his joyful scenes of Parisian life, his portraits and his nudes. He kept painting until his death despite severe arthritis.
