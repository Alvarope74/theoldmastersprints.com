Famous paintings at the Van Gogh Museum: 2 masterpieces to see

The Van Gogh Museum in Amsterdam opened in 1973 and holds the world's largest collection of works by Vincent van Gogh, with some 200 paintings, 500 drawings and hundreds of letters, most of them from the family of his brother Theo.

Must-see works

The Potato Eaters

Vincent van Gogh · 1885

Five peasants share a supper of potatoes and coffee by the light of an oil lamp in a cottage in Nuenen, the Brabant village where Van Gogh’s family lived.

Wheatfield with Crows

Vincent van Gogh · 1890

A golden wheat field stretches under a stormy dark blue sky, crossed by a flock of black crows.
