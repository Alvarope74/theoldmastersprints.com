Famous paintings at the Rijksmuseum: 3 masterpieces to see

The Rijksmuseum is the national museum of the Netherlands. It was founded in 1800 and its current Amsterdam building, designed by Pierre Cuypers, opened in 1885. Its main gallery is built around Rembrandt's The Night Watch.

Must-see works

The Night Watch

Rembrandt · 1642

Its real title is “The Company of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch”.

The Milkmaid

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1658–1660

A maid pours milk from a jug into a bowl, absorbed in her task, in a kitchen lit by a window on the left.

The Jewish Bride

Rembrandt · c. 1665–1669

A couple embraces tenderly: he rests his hand on her breast and she covers it with hers.
