Ivan Aivazovsky: biography, paintings and prints

Feodosia (Crimea), 1817 – Feodosia, 1900

Marine painter born to an Armenian family in Crimea and baptised Hovhannes Aivazian. He studied at the Imperial Academy of Arts in Saint Petersburg and became the official artist of the Russian Navy. He painted from memory and left around 6,000 paintings over a career of almost 60 years. In 1880 he opened an art gallery in his house in Feodosia.
