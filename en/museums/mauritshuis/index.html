Famous paintings at the Mauritshuis: 3 masterpieces to see

The Mauritshuis is a 17th-century palace in The Hague, built for Johan Maurits of Nassau-Siegen, which has been a museum since 1822. It is small, but it holds key Dutch Golden Age works by Vermeer and Rembrandt.

Must-see works

Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1665

A girl turns her head towards us with her lips slightly parted.

The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp

Rembrandt · 1632

Dr Nicolaes Tulp explains the anatomy of the forearm to seven members of the Amsterdam surgeons’ guild.

View of Delft

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1660–1661

Delft seen from the south, across the harbour on the river Schie, with the Schiedam and Rotterdam gates, the rooftops and the sunlit tower of the New Church.
