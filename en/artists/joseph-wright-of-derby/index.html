Joseph Wright of Derby: biography, paintings and prints

Derby (Inglaterra), 1734 – Derby, 1797

English portrait and landscape painter, acclaimed as the first professional painter to express the spirit of the Industrial Revolution. The son of a Derby attorney, he trained in London under Thomas Hudson, the master of Joshua Reynolds. He is famous for scenes lit by a single candle or lamp, in strong chiaroscuro, showing the scientific experiments of the Enlightenment. He was a friend of Erasmus Darwin and close to the Lunar Society of Birmingham; Josiah Wedgwood and Richard Arkwright were among his patrons. After a stay in Italy he painted eruptions of Vesuvius.
