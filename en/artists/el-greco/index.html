El Greco: biography, paintings and prints

Creta, 1541 – Toledo, 1614

Doménikos Theotokópoulos, known as El Greco, a painter born in Venetian Crete who made almost all his work in Spain. He trained as an icon painter in the Cretan tradition and around 1567 went to Venice, where a letter calls him a disciple of Titian; in 1570 he settled in Rome. In 1577 he arrived in Toledo, where he lived until his death, and his paintings for Santo Domingo el Antiguo made his name in the city. His best-known work is The Burial of the Count of Orgaz. He signed his pictures with his name in Greek letters. His style, with elongated figures and intense colours, puzzled his contemporaries and fell into neglect; rediscovered between the 19th and early 20th centuries, he is now regarded as a precursor of both Expressionism and Cubism.
