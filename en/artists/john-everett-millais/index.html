John Everett Millais: biography, paintings and prints

Southampton, 1829 – Londres, 1896

English painter and co-founder, in 1848, of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood, which championed painting true to nature and in bright colours. He later became a successful portraitist and President of the Royal Academy.
