Gustav Klimt: biography, paintings and prints

Baumgarten (Viena), 1862 – Viena, 1918

Austrian painter, founder and first president of the Vienna Secession in 1897. He is known for his portraits of women and for his “golden period”, in which he combined paint with gold leaf and geometric ornament.
