Famous paintings at the Tretyakov Gallery: 3 masterpieces to see

The Tretyakov Gallery in Moscow began in 1856 as the private collection of the merchant Pavel Tretyakov, who gave it to the city in 1892. It is the leading collection of Russian art, from medieval icons to the avant-garde.

Must-see works

Black Square

Kazimir Malevich · 1915

A black square on a white ground.

Ivan the Terrible and his son Ivan on November 16, 1581

Ilya Repin · 1883–1885

Tsar Ivan IV holds his dying son, the Tsarevich Ivan, whom he has just struck on the head with his staff in a fit of rage on 16 November 1581.

Theotokos of Vladimir

Anonymous · early 12th century

The Virgin holds the Child, who presses his cheek against hers and puts an arm around her neck.
