Diego Velázquez: biography, paintings and prints

Sevilla, 1599 – Madrid, 1660

Court painter to Philip IV and the central figure of the Spanish Golden Age. He moved from the naturalism of his Seville works to a loose brushwork that, seen close up, looks almost abstract. Most of his work is in the Prado Museum.
